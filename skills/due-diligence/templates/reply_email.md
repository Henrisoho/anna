# Reply templates

Fill the brackets. Keep it short and warm; the request list does the work.

## 1. First reply (before we know the deal type)

Subject: Re: [their subject]

Hi [first name],

Thanks for reaching out. We'd be glad to take a look at [company / project].

To route this to the right review, could you tell us:

1. What the business or asset is, in one or two sentences.
2. What you're raising: amount, instrument (equity, debt, fund commitment, co-invest), and use of proceeds.
3. Stage and timeline: when you need to close, and whether there's a lead investor or lender.
4. Who the sponsor or management team is, and where the entity is organized.

Once we have that, we'll send a short first-stage request list.

Best,
[name]

## 2. Sending the packet (after intake)

Subject: [Deal name]: due diligence request list (Stage 1)

Hi [first name],

Thanks for the overview. We've classified [deal name] as a [deal type] deal and attached our Stage 1 request list.

How it works:

- **Stage 1 (attached)** covers the basics we review before anything else: [3 to 5 headline items, e.g. identity
  and entity documents, the model, the deck, 36 months of bank statements, and authorizations for us to verify
  directly with your bank and run background checks]. Items marked GATING must be satisfied to proceed.
- When Stage 1 clears, we'll send the next stages (term sheet, confirmatory, third-party reports, closing).
- Please upload documents to [data room link] in folders named with each request number, and mark anything that
  doesn't apply as N/A with a one-line reason.
- We verify key documents directly with the issuer (banks, auditors, administrators, counterparties). That's
  standard for every deal we review and isn't a reflection on you.

There's also a short management questionnaire at the end. Written answers first, then we'll set up a call.

Happy to walk through any of it.

Best,
[name]

## 3. Declining

Best,
