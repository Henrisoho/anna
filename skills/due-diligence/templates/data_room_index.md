# Data room folder structure

Ask the sponsor to use this structure. Each document goes in its workstream folder, named with its request number
from the request list (for example `04 Financial/U-FIN-030 Bank statements 2023-2026.pdf`).

```
00 Index and request tracker
01 Sponsor & Background
02 KYC-AML & Sanctions
03 Corporate & Legal
04 Financial
05 Tax
06 Commercial & Market
07 Operational
08 Technical
09 Collateral & Title
10 Regulatory & Compliance
11 Environmental & ESG
12 Insurance
13 IT & Cyber
14 HR & Management
15 Structure & Terms
16 Valuation & Model
17 Fund Operations
18 Third-party reports
19 Management questionnaire answers
20 Closing deliverables
```

Rules for the sponsor:

- One document per file; no zipped bundles.
- Bank statements, audits, confirmations and third-party reports must be the issuer's original PDF.
- Spreadsheets live as spreadsheets (with formulas), not PDFs, so we can recompute.
- Do not delete or replace a document after upload; upload a new version with `v2` in the name.
- Watermarks are fine. Password-protected files must have the password in the index.

Rules for us:

- Log every upload and every question in the tracker with the date.
- Turn every hint in the data room (a reference to a lost customer, a cancelled contract, a dispute, a volume decline)
  into a written question and record the answer (bible 0.16, Paragon Metals).
