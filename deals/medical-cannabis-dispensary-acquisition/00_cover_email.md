**How the process works**

- **Part 1 (attached): screen.** 32 items. The headline ones:
  - a one-page summary of the raise and the acquisition;
  - sources and uses;
  - the live financial model, including a version with federal tax rebuilt at full Section 280E;
  - historical financials for [Company name] and the target;
  - formation documents and an ownership chart down to every licensed entity;
  - beneficial-ownership certification;
  - a schedule of every state and local cannabis license.
- **Part 2: before a term sheet.** The 280E position and any Schedule III or medical relief claimed, the downside
  case, deal terms and the management questionnaire.
- **Part 3: confirmatory diligence and third-party reports.** Proof of cash, seed-to-sale reconciliation, licensing,
  tax, legal, employees, IT, insurance, and access for our quality-of-earnings provider and tax counsel.
- **Part 4: closing and post-close reporting.** The purchase agreement, regulator approval of the change of control,
  and ongoing reporting.

We send each part once the previous one has been reviewed and cleared.

**A few notes**

- Where an item refers to the "target", it means [target dispensary]. Everything else refers to [Company name] and
  its licensed entities. If an item applies to both, please provide both.
- Items marked GATING must be satisfied for us to proceed. Two matter most at this stage:
  - **The 280E model.** Please model federal tax as if no ordinary deductions are allowed. Only medical revenue
    backed by a DEA registration, a tax opinion and a defensible apportionment can be credited with relief.
    Adult-use cannabis remains Schedule I.
  - **The licenses.** We will confirm license standing and change-of-control approval directly with the state
    regulator.
- We verify bank statements, vault and armored-carrier records, and licenses directly with the issuer. That is
  standard for every deal we review. The authorization forms are in Part 1.
- Please upload documents to [data room link] in folders named with each request number. Mark anything that doesn't
  apply as N/A with a one-line reason.
