- Linneman "What Should Stabilized Multifamily Cap Rates Be?" Zell/Lurie Review (~2005), WP 519: forward CF cap = r - g; NOI cap = CF cap/(1-d); recurring capex/NOI: MF 16-18% (17), retail 36, industrial 31, office 33 (NCREIF); risk premium A=BB ~210bp, B=BB- ~260bp, C=B ~350bp over 10y UST; g~2%; sustainable fwd CF spread A~0, B~60bp, C~150bp; CF cap 5-5.5% ~ unlevered 7-7.5%; Table II at 4.2% UST: NOI cap A 5.1 B 5.7 C 6.7; at 5% UST: 6.0/6.6/7.7; unlevered apt beta ~0.5; industry confuses NOI vs CF cap rates.

- Kleinman "How PJM Is Grappling With Data Center Power Demand" (Feb 3 2026; Silverman JHU, Rutigliano NRDC): PJM capacity bills ~$2.2B (2023+2024) -> >$14B one year; ~$40B over 3 yrs, ~half attributed to data centers (IMM); collar: $16B cleared vs ~$26B uncapped; ~6.5 GW/yr DC load through 2030; half of projects leaving queue face 4-7 yr waits for transmission upgrades; turbine lead times 4-6 yrs; Jan 16 2026 PJM board letter: BYOG, connect-and-manage/curtailment (fallback to diesel), backstop auction.
